## The point

- **No ads** — no banners, no cookie consent walls, no newsletter pop-ups.
- **No servers** — everything runs inside your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and with no round trip you get instant results.
- **No noise** — no login, no sign-up. Just open it and use it.

## Tools

- **QR code** — generate QR codes for URLs, text, email, phone, SMS, WiFi, contacts (vCard), location, events, and app links. Rich styling with colors, gradients, dot shapes, and a center logo. Export as PNG, SVG, JPEG, or WebP.
- **Image redact** — hide parts of an image. Drag to mark regions, then blur, pixelate, or black them out, and export as PNG.
