# 404 Not Found

**Nothing but developer tools.**

🔗 **[oxvx.github.io](https://oxvx.github.io)**

## The point

- **No ads** — no banners, no cookie consent walls, no newsletter pop-ups.
- **No signup** — no account, nothing to install. Open a tool and use it.
- **No uploads** — every tool runs in your browser; your input is never uploaded.
- **No limits** — no daily cap, no paid tier, no file-count gate.

## Tools

- **QR code** — generate QR codes for URLs, text, email, phone, SMS, WiFi, contacts (vCard), location, events, and app links. Colors, gradients, dot and corner shapes, and a center logo. Export as PNG, SVG, JPEG, or WebP.
- **Image markup** — hide parts of a screenshot or any image. Drag to mark regions, then blur, pixelate, or black them out. Exported images have their EXIF/GPS metadata removed. Export as PNG.
- **Favicon** — turn text, an emoji, or an image into a whole favicon set: a multi-size `.ico`, eight PNG sizes from 16 to 512 including the apple-touch-icon, and the HTML snippet, one at a time or as a zip. Text can run to two lines, each with its own color, over one background or two split bands, in any Google Fonts typeface.
- **OG image** — build the 1200×630 card a shared link shows. A title and a subtitle wrap to fit on their own and keep any line breaks you type, aligned left, center, or right, over a solid or gradient background, with a photo filling the card or a logo placed above or beside the text. Export as PNG or JPEG, and copy the meta tags to paste.
- **My IP** — see your public IPv4 and IPv6 addresses and copy them as they are or as `/32` and `/128` blocks for an allow list. Your browser finds them by asking Cloudflare's STUN server (Google's as a fallback); no IP lookup API is called.
